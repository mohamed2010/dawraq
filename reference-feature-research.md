# Reference Feature Research

## Scope note

No authoritative public listing for an Arabic menstrual tracking application named exactly "متابعتي" was located in the initial search. The feature baseline below therefore comes from a publicly available Arabic period-tracker listing and must not be represented as an exact feature inventory of the user's reference application without a direct link or screenshots.

## Public feature baseline

The Arabic Google Play listing for **Cylo: Period, Ovulation & Cycle Tracker** describes the following user-facing feature categories:

- Period and bleeding logging, cycle-phase predictions, ovulation, and fertility estimates.
- Daily health observations, including mood, symptoms, weight, and temperature.
- Private reminders for period dates, fertility, contraception medication, and daily mood/symptom logging.
- Privacy controls, offline use, backup and restore, and data deletion requests.
- Educational content covering periods, ovulation, contraception, fertility, and pregnancy.
- Different support modes for teenagers, people trying to conceive, people avoiding pregnancy, and general wellness tracking.

Zuhaira already includes account-isolated profiles, ongoing and completed period logging, cycle calculations, calendar, daily mood and symptom tracking, themes, stealth mode, and an offline guidance chat. The primary candidate additions are therefore: user-configurable reminders, richer daily health measurements (weight/temperature/flow/discharge), export/backup, a structured educational hub, and optional goal modes. Any fertility or pregnancy content must stay clearly informational and avoid medical diagnosis.
